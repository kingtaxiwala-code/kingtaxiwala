Hyderabad to Vijayawada Route Details

Everything you need to know about this popular intercity route

The Journey

The Hyderabad to Vijayawada route covers approximately 300 km via National Highway 65 (NH65). This well-maintained highway passes through Nalgonda, Suryapet, and Nandigama before reaching Vijayawada. The journey typically takes 6 hours depending on traffic conditions.

Key Stops

  • Hyderabad (Start)
  • Nalgonda
  • Suryapet
  • Kodad
  • Nandigama
  • Vijayawada (End)

Travel Tips

  • Best time: Early morning (5–7 AM)
  • Carry valid ID for toll plazas
  • Food stops at Suryapet & Kodad
  • Highway has good rest areas
  • Book in advance for peak seasons
