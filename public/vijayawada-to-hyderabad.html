Vijayawada to Hyderabad Route Details

Everything you need to know about this popular intercity route

The Journey

The Vijayawada to Hyderabad route covers approximately 300 km via National Highway 65 (NH65). This well-maintained highway passes through Nandigama, Suryapet, and Nalgonda before reaching Hyderabad. The journey typically takes 6 hours depending on traffic conditions.

Key Stops

  • Vijayawada (Start)
  • Nandigama
  • Kodad
  • Suryapet
  • Nalgonda
  • Hyderabad (End)

Travel Tips

  • Best time: Early morning (5–7 AM)
  • Carry valid ID for toll plazas
  • Food stops at Nandigama & Kodad
  • Highway has good rest areas
  • Book in advance for peak seasons
